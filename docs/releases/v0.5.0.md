**Signed and notarized for Apple Silicon Macs.** Open the DMG, drag FreeGaz to Applications, and click **Open** at the usual "downloaded from the Internet" prompt.

## FreeGaz now updates itself

This is the last version you'll need to download by hand. From here on, FreeGaz keeps itself up to date:

- **It checks GitHub's releases** a little after launch and every six hours, and downloads a new version in the background.
- **Nothing restarts on its own.** The update installs the next time you quit, or straight away from **Restart to update** in the sidebar. That notice is hidden during a ride.
- **Settings → About → Updates** shows the status and has **Check for updates**. There's also a switch to turn automatic updates off, and one for pre-releases (every 0.x release is one).
- **The check only asks GitHub which release is newest.** It sends nothing about you or your rides.

Coming from 0.4.0 or earlier? Download this version from [freegaz.app](https://freegaz.app) and drag it over the old one. Your rides and settings stay. Coming from 0.3.0 or earlier, macOS also asks once more for Bluetooth and for "FreeGaz Safe Storage" in the keychain; choose **Always Allow**.
