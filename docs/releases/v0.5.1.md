**Signed and notarized for Apple Silicon Macs.** On 0.5.0, FreeGaz updates itself: it installs the next time you quit, or straight away from **Restart to update** in the sidebar. Coming from 0.4.0 or earlier, download it from [freegaz.app](https://freegaz.app) and drag it over the old one.

## Contribute to the developer's bike fund

FreeGaz is free and always will be. If it saved you a subscription, you can now put a little toward the developer's next bike, through **Venmo** or **Buy Me a Coffee**:

- **In the sidebar,** a small **Bike fund** link under Report a bug (on a phone, it's in **More**).
- **In Settings → About,** a **Bike fund** row.

Each button opens the tip page in your browser. It's entirely optional, and it unlocks nothing.
