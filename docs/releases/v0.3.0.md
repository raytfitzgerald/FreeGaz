**Ad-hoc signed, un-notarized build for Apple Silicon Macs.** On first launch, right-click FreeGaz.app → Open, or run `xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/FreeGaz.app` (see the README's Install section).

## What's new since 0.2.0

**Riding**
- **Pick a ride.** A big Ride button tops the sidebar, and the Ride tab asks what kind of ride: for time, free ride, route or workout, with the trainer and heart-rate status up front.
- **Ride for time.** 20 to 90 minutes (or any length), the trainer holding a steady wattage or your own pace, with an optional ease in and out.
- **The ride-along.** Your coach bikes next to you on a little road, pacing at your target, and says their lines in a speech bubble. Fold it or turn it off; mute the coach from the ride screen.

**Coaching**
- **Profanity in three levels:** Clean, Mild and Unhinged (with 188 new unhinged lines). Spice 5 is now called Feral.
- **Cartoon faces** for the eight character coaches.
- **The coach chat answers in character**, at your spice and profanity levels. Grok (xAI) joins Claude, OpenAI and Ollama.

**Home, history and workouts**
- **Home is a velodrome:** this week's rides lap the track, your coach shouts from the infield, and before your first ride of the week a rival coach does laps instead.
- **Your name and photo** put your own face on your rider.
- **Fitness overview:** a plain-English read of your fitness, with Ask the coach.
- **History calendar:** a month at a time, alongside the list.
- **Workouts in three tabs:** FreeGaz workouts, Custom workouts and a numbered, reorderable Training plan. The builder files new workouts in either.
- **Speed and weight units** of their own (km/h or mph, kg or lb).
- **Report a bug** from the sidebar opens a pre-filled GitHub issue.

## Fixes
- Settings changes no longer reset other settings (Light mode flipping back to System).
- Body & heart numbers (and FTP) can be edited again, and quick edits no longer overwrite each other.
- Pause-and-resume within a second no longer stops recording; a double click on Start no longer starts two rides.
- A failed save offers the ride for recovery straight away; the journal writes its last records before closing; recovered rides keep their HR zones.
- Rides started after the Mac slept are dated correctly.
- Very long route or workout names no longer make a ride unsaveable.
- The ride detail page copes with incomplete ride records.
